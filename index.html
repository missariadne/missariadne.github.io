The part nobody warns you about

A finished plan is not the same thing as a finishable draft.

Most planning advice stops at the outline. You get a beat sheet, a premise, a tidy list of what happens, and then the instructions run out exactly where the work starts.

So the outline gets rebuilt instead. A fourth version, smoother than the third. Smooth prose is extremely good at hiding a structural problem. It reads fine. It does not do anything.

That is not a discipline problem, and it is not a sign you picked the wrong book. It is a missing step between the plan and the page.
