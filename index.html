Concept Clarity

Character Foundation

Build deep character psychology by answering: Who is your protagonist? What do they want in the world and inside themselves? Using the Character Core Beliefs Workbook and Flawed Characters Workbook with Blueprint Part 2, you'll infuse your protagonist with cognitive distortions and authentic depth.

Flexible Structure

Connect cause and effect throughout your story. Using Save the Cat beats, the Snowflake Method, and Blueprint's "Because of That" Story Summary, you'll build a beat sheet and conflict map that shows how each story moment causes the next.
