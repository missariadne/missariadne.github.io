## Key Points Up Front

1. **It's not social media, it's identity.** People stop scrolling when they see themselves. Content should work like a mirror.
2. **Hooks have to be relevant, recognizable, and familiar.** "4 tax tips" is out. "Make $100K–$250K? Here's your year-end tax tip" is in.
3. **Get to the feeling, not the outcome.** Keep asking "why does that matter?" until you hit the emotion.
4. **Use their words.** Use what your audience calls themselves. Nobody calls themselves a "homeowner."
5. **Borrow familiarity.** Name brands, stores, places, movies, and eras people already know (Walmart, Starbucks, Canva, an early-2000s holiday movie).
6. **Place matters,** especially for anything local.
7. **No fancy edits, no talking needed.** A 6-second B-roll clip with trending audio and a great hook beats a polished edit.
8. **Test like crazy.** Try 5 trial reels a day. A bad trial doesn't mean it's a bad post. When a hook works, keep reusing it until it stops.
9. **We're in the "insider era."** People want to see what the Starbucks barista orders or what the HomeGoods worker found. That's the kind of thing AI can't fake.

## The Engagement Formula (her checklist slide)

> **Name a feeling · a moment · a place · an identity · something timely**

The editor should check every hook against these five.

---

## Her 4H Content Mix

| H | Meaning | Feeling it creates |
|---|---|---|
| **Helpful** | Education and tips | "That's for me, something useful is coming" |
| **Heard** | Lived experience and naming what they feel | "Seen. Sending this to my group chat." |
| **Humorous** | Light, cheeky, silly | A dopamine hit, shareable |
| **Happenings** | Behind the scenes of your business | "I know what it's like to walk in there" |

## The 5 Posts + Bonus (hook templates)

| # | Format | Hook skeleton | Video | Length |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Patiently Waiting** | "Patiently waiting for all the [identity in THEIR words] who [specific feeling or lived detail] to find my page so [payoff]" | One visual-proof shot + trending audio | 6–12 sec ("one-page reel") |
| 2a | **POV: You Need** | "POV: you need [thing] because [scenario they're in right now]" | Quick B-roll, jump cuts of 1 sec or less | Until the payoff |
| 2b | **POV: You Discovered** | "POV: you discovered [place/product] that [oddly specific detail]" | Experience B-roll that invites them in | Short |
| 3 | **Cheeky Proposal** | "I'm not saying we should [skip the responsible thing] and [do the fun thing]… but" | Existing camera-roll clip. Can propose something that doesn't exist yet. | 6–10 sec |
| 4 | **Things I Do (carousel)** | "[N] things I do to [desire] as a [identity + situation]" | Camera-roll photos + IG text, first person | 5–8 slides |
| 5 | **"And for the ___, perhaps a ___"** | "And for the [identity], perhaps a [tasteful, specific thing]" (trending movie-quote riff) | Show each item | 8–15 sec |
| ★ | **Rapid Fire (talk to camera)** | "Rapid fire: [N] [things] for when you're [pain point]" | Filmed in a recognizable place, text overlays | 20–40 sec |

**Why these work:** together they introduce your account, give value, invite people into your world, and show what you offer. Shannon calls them "the first five posts I'd make as your social media manager."

## Features to Build Into the Editor

| # | Feature | What it does | Principle |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Audience Profile panel** (saved, switchable) | Fields: what they call themselves · age/life stage · places (local spots + stores they shop) · taste words · current moments (season, holidays, launches) · feelings and pain points | "Know your audience is #1" |
| 2 | **Why Ladder** | Outcome → why? → why? → **feeling**. The final rung goes into the hook. | Feeling, not outcome |
| 3 | **Hook Builder** | The 6 templates above, with slots that autofill from the Audience Profile | Proven formats |
| 4 | **Feel-Seen Checklist / Score** | Checks the hook for a feeling, moment, place, identity, timeliness, and borrowed familiarity. Flags generic words ("readers," "authors," "tips," "homeowners") and jargon. | Relevant, recognizable, familiar |
| 5 | **Borrow-Familiarity Bank** | A pick-list of brands, stores, places, movies, eras, and tools your people know | Borrowed familiarity |
| 6 | **4H tag + weekly balance meter** | Tag each reel H1–H4 and warn when the week is all Helpful | 4H mix |
| 7 | **Format presets** | One-page reel (text on screen the whole time, 6 sec) · POV B-roll (1-sec cut guide) · Carousel (camera roll + text) · Rapid-fire talk to camera with overlays | No fancy edits |
| 8 | **Trial Reel Variations** | Generates 5 versions of one hook by swapping the identity, place, or feeling. Logs results and marks each hook "working," "testing," or "retired." | Test, tweak, rinse and repeat |
| 9 | **Insider / Happenings prompt deck** | "Most-ordered thing this week," "what's on my desk," "what I'd grab if I were you" | Insider era, storytelling isn't deep |
| 10 | **Seasonal moment hooks** | A calendar of timely moments (holidays, NaNoWriMo, winter, back to school, Black Friday) to drop into hooks | Timely = relevant |
